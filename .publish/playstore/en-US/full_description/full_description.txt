OpenPlayer is a free, open-source music player for Android with no ads and no tracking. It plays the music already on your phone and never connects to the internet.

This is an early alpha. Today it can:
- Scan your device for music
- Browse your music by folder
- Play songs with play/pause, next/previous, repeat and shuffle

Planned: artists and albums, playlists, equalizer, lyrics, notification controls and gapless playback.
