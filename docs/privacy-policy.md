# Privacy Policy

## OpenPlayer - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-20

### Summary

OpenPlayer is a privacy-first music player for Android. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline on your device.

### Information We Collect

**None. OpenPlayer collects zero user data.**

- No personal information
- No usage analytics
- No crash reports
- No telemetry
- No advertising IDs
- No location data
- No network activity

### Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:
- Music library metadata (song titles, artists, albums, etc.) — read from your audio files
- Play counts and last played timestamps
- Playlists and queue positions
- Equalizer presets and device profiles
- Settings and preferences

### Permissions

OpenPlayer requires the following permissions for local functionality only:

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| READ_MEDIA_AUDIO | Access audio files on your device (Android 13+) |
| READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE | Read music files (Android 12 and below) |
| FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK | Background playback |
| WAKE_LOCK | Keep playing with the screen off |

### Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally:
- Delete the app to remove all data
- Delete individual songs/playlists/queues from within the app
- Backups can be deleted from the file system

### Third-Party Services

OpenPlayer uses no third-party services. All functionality is implemented locally. No Google Play Services, Firebase, or other external services are required.

### Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be published with the app update. The policy date above reflects the last revision.
